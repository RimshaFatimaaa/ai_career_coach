# Career development and skill gaps

A useful career plan starts from an actual profile, not a generic blog list. Compare current skills to a target role. Grade each skill Strong, Intermediate, Beginner, or Missing. Rank gaps by how blocking they are for that role.

Turn high and medium gaps into a 1, 3, 6, or 12 month roadmap. Each milestone needs a skill, an objective, a resource, a small exercise, a project, and an expected result. Users should be able to tick tasks, add personal tasks, and change deadlines.

Readiness percentages are internal estimates for motivation and sequencing. They are not employment probabilities.

Students often over-index on courses and under-index on projects that prove the skill. Career switchers should map transferable skills explicitly instead of hiding the previous field.
