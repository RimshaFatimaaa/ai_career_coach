# ATS scoring and keyword alignment

ATS readiness estimates look at keyword overlap, skill coverage, section completeness, and simple formatting signals. They are not a promise that any particular employer system will rank you highly.

Job descriptions mix required skills, optional skills, and corporate boilerplate. Extract nouns that look like tools, languages, and methods. Compare them to the resume text. Missing keywords should be flagged — not silently inserted.

Formatting checks: contact email present, standard section names, readable dates, no empty experience bullets.

Always label scores as estimates for personal tracking.
