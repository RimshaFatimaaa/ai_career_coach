# Personalized reminders

Reminders are generated from incomplete roadmap tasks and weak interview scores, or created by hand.

- Incomplete tasks with a deadline become reminders.
- Interview scores below 70 prompt a short rehearsal reminder.
- Completing a reminder does not complete the roadmap task; do both if they are linked.

These are coaching nudges, not calendar guarantees. Email is not sent unless you later connect SMTP.
