# STAR method for behavioral interviews

Behavioral questions probe past behavior as a proxy for future performance. Structure answers as:

- Situation: one or two sentences of context
- Task: what you specifically owned
- Action: what you did, with enough technical or interpersonal detail
- Result: what changed. Prefer numbers you actually have; otherwise describe a qualitative outcome honestly

Weak answers skip the result, speak in "we" without your role, or ramble without a decision. A good follow-up is: "What was the measurable result?" or "What would you do differently?"

Common prompts: conflict, failure, ambiguity, influence without authority, why this role.
